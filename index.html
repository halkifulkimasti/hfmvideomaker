How to Use HFM Video Maker

HFM Video Maker is a next-generation AI content creation suite built for digital creators, animators, and storytellers to generate watermark-free short videos and realistic multi-age voiceovers in Hindi, English, and major global languages.

1. Creating Watermark-Free AI Videos

  • Choose Your AI Engine: Select Seedance 1.0 for fluid character motion, MiniMax (Hailuo Video-01) for cinematic 3D visuals, or RunwayML (Gen-3 Alpha Turbo) for image-driven animation.
  • Write a Detailed Prompt: Describe the character's appearance, clothing, background environment, and action clearly.
  • Reference Image (Optional): Upload a character reference image to maintain visual consistency across multiple scenes, or leave it blank for pure Text-to-Video generation.

2. Generating Multi-Age & Multi-Language Voiceovers

  • Choose Sarvam AI for authentic Indian regional voices (Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi) or ElevenLabs for expressive global and cartoon voices.
  • Select the desired speaker age and gender, enter your script, and download studio-grade audio instantly.

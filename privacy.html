🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

At HFM Voice Maker, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed while you use our AI text-to-speech studio.

1. Scripts & Audio Processing

The text scripts you enter into HFM Voice Maker are transmitted securely to our AI voice synthesis partners (Sarvam AI and ElevenLabs) solely to generate your requested audio output. We do not permanently store or publicly publish your private scripts or generated audio files on our servers.

2. Local Browser Storage

We use browser localStorage to save your subscription preference, notification settings, and user reviews locally on your device for a smoother user experience.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Cookies

We may display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdSense or partner ad networks) to keep HFM Voice Maker free for creators. Third-party vendors may use cookies or web beacons to serve relevant ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage or disable cookies anytime through your browser settings.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at halkifulkimasti@gmail.com.

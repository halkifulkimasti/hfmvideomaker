Privacy Policy — HFM Video Maker

Last Updated: November 2026

At HFM Video Maker (accessible at hfmvideomaker.netlify.app), protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how we use it.

1. Media & Prompt Processing

Text prompts and optional reference images submitted by users are processed solely through official AI inference APIs to generate requested video or audio outputs. We do not sell, rent, or trade user media to third parties.

2. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our website and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.

3. Log Files & Analytics

Standard server log files (such as browser type, ISP, date/time stamp, and referring pages) may be recorded for security and performance optimization.

4. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, contact us at halkifulkimasti@gmail.com.

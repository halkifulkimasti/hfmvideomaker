How to Use HFM Video Maker — Creator Guide

HFM Video Maker helps creators generate watermark-free AI videos and realistic multi-age voiceovers in Hindi, English, and major global languages.

Step 1: Generating Watermark-Free AI Videos

  • Select Your Video Engine: Choose Seedance 1.0 for smooth character movement, MiniMax (Hailuo Video-01) for cinematic 3D animation, or RunwayML (Gen-3 Alpha Turbo) for image-to-video motion.
  • Enter a Detailed Prompt: Describe the character, outfit, expression, lighting, and background clearly.
  • Upload Reference Image (Optional): Upload a character image to keep your character consistent across multiple scenes, or leave it empty for Text-to-Video.
  • Download HD MP4: Once rendered, preview and download your clean, watermark-free MP4 video.

Step 2: Creating Multi-Age & Multi-Language Voiceovers

  • Switch to Voice Studio on the home page.
  • Choose Sarvam AI for natural Indian languages (Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi) or ElevenLabs for global and cartoon voices.
  • Pick a speaker matching your character's age and gender, paste your script, and download studio-quality audio.

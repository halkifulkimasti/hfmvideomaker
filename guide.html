📘 How to Use HFM Voice Maker

Follow these simple steps to generate natural, studio-quality AI voiceovers in Hindi, English, and 10+ regional languages for YouTube videos, Shorts, Reels, and stories.

Step 1: Choose Your Voice Engine

  • Sarvam AI (Indian Multi-Age): Best for natural Hindi, English (Indian accent), Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, and Punjabi voices without robotic distortion.
  • ElevenLabs (Global & Cartoon): Best for expressive global narrations, deep storytelling voices, and animated cartoon characters.

Step 2: Select Language & Speaker Profile

Choose your Target Language and select a Speaker matching your character's age and gender—including Young Male, Mature/Elder Male, Young Female, Elder Female, and Kids voices.

Step 3: Paste Your Script (No Character Limit)

Type or paste your dialogue, story, or YouTube script into the Dialogue / Script box. Even if your script is longer than 500 characters, HFM Voice Maker automatically splits it into natural sentences and merges the audio seamlessly.

Step 4: Generate & Download Audio

Tap Generate Studio Voice, preview your voiceover directly in the built-in player, and tap Download Audio to save the high-clarity WAV or MP3 file to your device.
